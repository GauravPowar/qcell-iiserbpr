About Qcell.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To cultivate a vibrant quizzing culture on campus by providing a platform for students to learn, compete, and share their passion for knowledge.

Vision: To be recognized as the most dynamic and inclusive student club, fostering intellectual curiosity and building a community of lifelong learners.

A Brief History

Founded in 2016 by a small group of trivia enthusiasts, Qcell started with a simple goal: to create a space for students who love to learn. Our first major event, Quizzard Premier League (QPL) the grand Intra-College Quiz Fest, set the stage for what would become a campus-wide phenomenon. Today, we are proud to be one of the most active clubs, carrying forward a legacy of curiosity and competition.

Council Affiliation & Inter-College Representation

Science Council Affiliation: Qcell operates officially under the Science Council, IISER Berhampur, promoting critical thinking, trivia, and competitive learning.

Representing IISER Berhampur: Qcell proudly represents the institute at prestigious inter-college quiz competitions including the Inter-IISER Cultural Meet, Mettle Meet, and major national trivia events.

What We Do

  • Weekly Themed Quizzes (Sci-Fi, History, Pop Culture, General Knowledge)
  • Annual Flagship Event: The Quiz Premiere League (QPL)
  • Representing IISER Berhampur at Inter-IISER Cultural Meet & Mettle Meet
  • Workshops on Quiz-Making, Question Design, and Quizzing Techniques
